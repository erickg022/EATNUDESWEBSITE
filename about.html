Tim & Ryan — Co-Founders

NUDES Health Bar

Founded by us in Barcelona, NUDES is a premium health bar providing fast, healthy nutrition in a fun environment.

When we started it was just two of us behind the bar. Now we are a few more but we have always tried to maintain that personal touch, as we know this is the most important part of your experience.

Real ingredients. Full transparency. Nothing hidden, nothing artificial. Fresh, nutrient-dense food made in-house with love every morning. We never have and we never will serve food that we are not happy to eat ourselves.

Whether it’s your morning coffee, breakfast for the office, pre or post-workout recharge, a quick lunch or dinner, we’re here to make healthy eating fun and easy.

Our goal was to create open design spaces that feel inviting, clean and somewhere you can sit with your friends or chat to the person next to you. Come for the food, stay for the vibe. Eat, connect, reset, leave happier than you arrived.

We couldn’t have done it without our amazing community - and the crew working tirelessly behind the scenes.

We’ve put everything into this, and all we hope is that you love it as much as we do.

(We have a feeling you will)

with love,

Tim & Ryan
